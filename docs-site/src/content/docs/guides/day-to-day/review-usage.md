The usage breakdown on **Audit & Costs** counts requests over the last 24
hours, 7 days or 30 days, split by outcome: served upstream, served from cache,
denied, errors, and queued. It groups by identity, source and team; requests
from identities without a team are counted under **No team**, so the groups
add up to the total.

These are counts, not costs; InterLock does not price requests. The cache hit
rate is measured over requests that were served, not over denials.
